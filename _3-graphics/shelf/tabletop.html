Table controls
LMB click / double-click
Draw / cut a packet in half. Holding LMB only picks up and moves.
RMB click / double-click
Flip a packet / shuffle it. A single card touching a matching deck goes into a random position / onto the touching top or bottom, matching its facing. Hold RMB to gather loose matching cards.
Wheel while holding
Cards: raise/lower. Other objects: rotate on the board. Wheel over empty table zooms.
Q / E · F · R
Rotate · flip · return a selected object. Pickup straightens its bottom.
Arrow keys
Move a selected object. Shift gives finer movement. Use the selector above to choose any object without a mouse.
