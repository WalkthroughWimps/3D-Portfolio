Mouse controls
TapDouble-tapHold / dragScroll

LMB hold

  • Drag a piece or an overlapping hand.
  • Tap RMB to flip a held card or roll a held die.
  • Double-tap RMB to shuffle the held packet.

RMB hold on a card

  • Tap LMB to fan or stack the packet.
  • Hold both buttons to gather loose matching cards. Hands and other stacks stay put.

Cards & view

  • Tap RMB to flip. Double-tap to shuffle.
  • Scroll over a card to flip in that direction. Each flip finishes before another starts.
  • Tap a packet to draw to the table. Tap a fanned card to slide it out; drop it on a hand to add it.
  • RMB drag on empty table rotates the view. Cards block view rotation.
  • Middle drag pans. Scroll over empty table to zoom.
Keyboard & buttons

Use the piece selector and buttons below without mouse chords.

H: fan/stack · F: flip · Q/E: rotate · R: return · arrows: move a selected piece. Escape cancels a hold.
