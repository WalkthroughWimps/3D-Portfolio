# Dog bark keyboard samples

`dog-bark-01.wav` through `dog-bark-04.wav` are short excerpts of [Dog Barking Mono](https://opengameart.org/content/dog-barking-mono), created by Brandon Morris and submitted by HaelDB. `dog-bark-05.wav` through `dog-bark-08.wav` come from [Dog Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/dog-sounds) by pauliuw. Both source pages offer the recordings under CC0 1.0.

The eight files are mono 22,050 Hz PCM WAV, each about 0.21–0.39 seconds. They were cropped from the originals, filtered, normalized, and given short edge fades. The player rotates through the eight recordings across adjacent keys and changes playback rate to approximate the requested note. Bark pitches are rough estimates; these are varied real barks, not 61 separately recorded or naturally tuned notes.
