## Face ownership and shared-file reservations

- Before editing or moving project source, configuration, documentation, or assets, reserve every intended file (or its face-owned directory) with `node tools/face-file-reservations.mjs acquire --owner "<chat title>" --files <paths...>`. Acquire the complete set together; do not hold one set while waiting for another. Initial creation of the helper was the bootstrap exception.
- If acquisition reports a conflict, do not write those paths. Work elsewhere, or retry with `--wait-ms 30000`. After acquiring, reread the current files; earlier reads may be stale. Ordinary read-only inspection may continue while another chat holds a reservation.
- Keep the returned token. Renew at least every ten minutes with `renew --token <token>` and release with `release --token <token>` after finishing the edit/verification slice, including on failure. Reservations expire after fifteen minutes but are not automatically stolen. Recover an expired token only after checking that its chat has stopped editing, using `recover --token <token> --reason "<evidence>"`.
- Read `_8-tools-and-notes/about-face-coordination.md` before changing About faces. Each Face chat owns its `_1-about/faces/<face>/` entry. Notes also owns `_1-about/notes/`; Skills also owns `_7-shared-scripts/panel-assembly/`. Keep shared cube, registry, global settings, HTML/CSS and combined-model changes brief and explicitly reserved. Do not overwrite another chat's current work.
- Face modules borrow the cube model and shared camera/renderer. They own and dispose their added resources and implement the common attach/update/suspend/resume/dispose contract. Shared model geometry/materials are disposed by the cube host.
- The reservation helper's own transient state and disposable validation output do not require reservations. Reservations coordinate participating chats; they do not replace source review or block external editors at the operating-system level.
