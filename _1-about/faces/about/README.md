# About orbital picture rings

The About face defaults to 24 rigid annuli and an artwork-continuation center disc. The default outer diameter is 1.10 cube units, inner disc diameter 0.34, and ring gap zero for continuous readable artwork. Controls allow 1–60 rings, outer diameter 0.60–1.70, inner diameter 0.08–1.30, and gaps up to 0.03; normalization clamps the inner diameter and gap so every ring retains positive radial width. The eight supplied perspective scenes span one Cartesian atlas per picture. No portrait or shrine artwork is drawn, and the old Docs die is neither loaded nor rendered. The opposite side uses a separate, stable abstract atlas.

Each ring keeps its center on the face. During a picture change it orbits in the face plane through 360–480° while flipping about a carried in-plane hinge through exactly 180°. Its orbit destination is chosen once from the midpoint radius: inverse radius is mapped to 480° for the innermost ring and 360° for the outermost, with paired counterrotation. Small, fixed hinge offsets make neighboring paths distinct. This is a gear-inspired visual rhythm, not an exact mechanical gear ratio. The center disc makes a deliberate 180° side flip. Ring dimensions and centers never change during a turn; carrier depth stays at its clearance position until every pose is flat.

Both physical sides of every ring have independent materials. During the transition, the target artwork is placed on the opposite side only when that side's actual world normal points away from the camera (dot product below −0.35). The former front changes to abstract only under the same condition. A turn waits if camera motion prevents either hidden change. Once complete, physical side parity toggles. The final pivot quaternion supplies a two-dimensional UV transform for the artwork, including the mirrored orientation after odd half-turns, so all rings and the center align cleanly after repeated transitions. Reduced motion switches to a flat coherent front immediately.

Motion blur is a face-local, five-tap atlas sample on each ring face and the center. Each material samples only its own current front or abstract rear texture. The sampling offsets follow that piece's measured orbit and flip angle changes over real frame time, then pass through its existing endpoint UV transform. The flip component approximates projected foreshortening along the planned hinge; this is image-space softening, not a physical shutter exposure or silhouette trail. The Ring motion slider sets strength from 0 to 100% (default 60%), persisted with the other motion controls and clamped on archive import. UV spans are bounded, and every material is exactly sharp when its pose stops, waits flat for a hidden map swap, suspends, uses reduced motion, or is disposed. No duplicate translucent geometry or scene-wide postprocessing is used.

The default sequence is six-ring sets from outside in, with 2.8 seconds per set's ring motion and 1.2 seconds between set starts. Controls offer All at once, individual outside-in, inside-out or random ring order, fixed or random-sized sets, random or alternating set order, duration, stagger, easing, and minimum/maximum orbit travel within 360–480°. Random order and set sizes are chosen once per plan; there is no per-frame random motion. The easing menu has Linear, Smoothstep, Smootherstep, and in/out/in-out Sine, Quad, Cubic, Quart, Quint, Expo, and Circ curves. Random per ring samples one curve from a separately checked pool for each ring and the center when the plan starts; an empty pool uses Linear while leaving the user's unchecked choices intact. Angular progress is capped per update so even steep curves visit the hidden-side swap windows before finishing. Requests during a turn resolve to the latest picture revision. Geometry changes during a turn wait until every ring is flat, then rebuild face-owned resources with the current artwork. Controls persist locally and in version 8 of the About settings archive; version 2–7 picture imports remain compatible, and values are validated and clamped.

The Pictures section is last in About settings. Its eight supplied pictures and captions are editable collection entries; adding a picture creates a new entry that can use an imported PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF, or GIF, and blank captions leave pure artwork. Captions wrap automatically into up to three lines at a uniform font size for that picture; Enter preserves manual line breaks. The fitted layout avoids horizontal text squeezing and stays within the circular artwork. Names and captions can be previewed, saved, reordered, and removed. Ring clicks and Next picture cycle through the curated order and synchronize the editor selection. Imported images are resized to at most 1536 pixels on the longest edge, saved as alpha-preserving WebP blobs in browser IndexedDB under immutable asset keys, and decoded only when needed. Collection metadata and caption revisions persist in local storage and the version 8 archive. The archive carries local image references, not the binary files; on another browser or device a missing image is reported and needs reimport. An image or caption edit made during a turn is snapshotted and queued so the visible atlas does not change mid-turn.

The carrier now has a face-owned annular glass pane held by a narrow outer frame at the corrected circular opening. A fitted inner bezel and depth wall join its inner edge to the disc rim. Glass never spans behind the moving discs. The pane and bezel rebuild with every disc diameter edit, including the 0.60 and 1.70 limits. Their glow and glass strength follow the shared live contrast mapping. The whole display carrier smoothly compensates cube roll when Upright faces is enabled; ring pivots, texture transforms, physical-side gates, and blur remain independent.

Rest and turning depth come from transformed source triangles in the `back.about` backing and corrected chassis/lip/bearing. The flat disc seats 0.012 plus half its thickness ahead of the source triangle envelope within its own radius, with a small 0.010 focus lift. For a turn, each source triangle's projected distance from the center enters a spherical support bound for the complete rigid disc and sidewall; the carrier predeploys to that bound before a ring tilts, and returns after the poses flatten. The default measured local backing barrier is −0.282, flat rest depth −0.264, and full-turn depth +0.111, versus the previous always-focused +0.576. A larger diameter can require more outward travel to clear the real backing. Hysteresis prevents focus chatter. If focus is lost mid-turn, depth stays clear until all ring poses are flat. A pending hidden map change can wait with flat rings seated until the relevant side faces away. Unfocused picture requests wait for focus. The host's About framing proposal fits the ring sweep after a narrow viewport resize; active Notes framing keeps priority. The face borrows the cube, camera, renderer, and `back.about`, and disposes only its own meshes, materials, textures, listeners, and asynchronous load state.
