### Capacity advice — proposed, not implemented

Five-minute source clips and a three-minute playable reel are feasible. Current limits remain 30 seconds per source, 60 seconds of reel, 120 seconds of distinct source audio, 48 placements and 20 MB per import. Import errors already explain the duration, file-size, source-storage or free-space limit that rejected a clip.

A practical future target is 300 seconds per source, 180 seconds of reel, four voice buses, 48 placements, 20 minutes of distinct audio and a 100 MB import ceiling. The existing 24 kHz mono Float32 storage costs 28.8 MB per five-minute source, or 115.2 MB for four; copies share PCM. Decode of an uncompressed stereo file temporarily needs substantially more memory. The present base64 Float32 session format grows to about 38.4 MB per five-minute source before JSON/string overhead. Increasing capacities should therefore include compact session audio, byte-based active/history budgets, long-source In/Out selection and cached/windowed waveform drawing, plus desktop/mobile memory tests. A five-minute source may retain its full audio while only its selected in-reel portion plays. No capacity or session-encoding change is made in this pass.

## Earlier unfolded reel sizing study (before this reel revision)

The current .075-wide strips fold to .0375 on each spool (50% narrower). The current flange interior is about .167 deep. Sampling the **actual Oddio spheroid profile**, both reel locations, 48 angular positions, three radial and three axial sections, seven whole-reel tilts from −25° to +25°, and a .006 clearance allowance gives these approximate unfolded strip widths while preserving the current front envelope:

| Flange diameter change | Unfolded width | Narrower than current open cloth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current diameter | .0431 | 43% |
| 8% smaller | .0468 | 38% |
| 15% smaller | .0496 | 34% |
| 21% smaller | .0525 | 30% |

The best sampled whole-reel tilt is 0° in this constrained envelope. Tilting the complete flange pushes one rim forward and forces the hub rearward to preserve panel clearance. Individually splayed/staggered spools or a relieved front panel could produce a different result and remain design options. This study does not change the working reels, winding capacity or cloth paths. These are sampled visual clearances, not fabrication or continuous collision certification.

## Input, ownership and simulation

The upper physical panel retains REW, PLAY, STOP, REC/PRINT, FEED, loop, output mute, a current-voice mute and a test bell. The lower panel retains volume, MIX dials and EDIT counters/actions. Native file and microphone permission surfaces remain necessary; naming and exact numeric entry use small anchored DOM fields. The optional 2D console contains all audio tools and works independently of WebGL.

`face.js` exports the common lifecycle from `face-runtime.js`. The face borrows `back.odd`, the host camera/renderer and source model. It owns the added mechanism, textures, materials, controls, audio circuits and buffers. The host drives all attached updates; only the standalone console starts its own animation loop. Suspend stops playback/capture and decorative work. Detach disposes added 3D resources; final disposal releases UI and audio. Shared source geometry is never disposed by this face.

The four cloth ribbons use continuous analytic guide paths, a center crease compressed from .075 to .028 wide, procedural weave/normal textures and bounded damped bending. This is a guided visual ribbon model, without continuous stretch/shear or self-collision. The opposite reel reverses the lane order. Tape patches move left during playback; the supply unwinds and the take-up winds with radius following remaining fabric area. Winding and sewing feed are decorative and do not resample PCM or seek playback.

`arm-motion.mjs` supplies .40/.39 rigid-link poses. `atelier-queue.mjs` bounds pending jobs at six and remembers up to 64 clip IDs. Work snapshots include both old and new placement, and transitions retain deployed arms. Capsule/cylinder avoidance covers the other arm/tool and meter, with a raised helper path over the tabs. Cut-thread particles are capped at 256. All new geometry disposes through the transport's owned resource set.

The engine supplies post-volume RMS to the shared speaker-response helper; that helper owns its explicit face allowlist. Page/site mute, zero volume and suspended audio produce no drive. Reduced motion restores rest geometry and clears decorative arm routines.
