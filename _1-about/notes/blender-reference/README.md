# Notes glass and tablet modeling reference

Open `assets/about-cube/blend/notes-glass-reference.blend`. One Blender unit is one site scene unit; this project has no verified conversion to meters or millimeters. The imported `back.art` and `cube.main` source meshes are in collection 01. The site applies a small load-time circularization to the cube shell openings; that deformation is not baked into this reference shell. The labeled .895-radius opening guide gives the Notes aperture target. The circular glass, layer stack, bezel, and fixed seat use the site dimensions. The pane diameter is 1.670; opening radius .895; moving bezel radius .900. Its mount is at site cube-local `[-.966,0,0]` and outward is `-X`. Glass center is `[-1.011,0,0]` at rest and `[-1.191,0,0]` at full lift. Thus true outward glass travel is .180. Extra rear panes are hidden initially because the runtime shows them only for documents with enough layers.

Frames 1–10 show maximum outward carrier lift at the code's .25-second travel cap (30 fps). Frames 1–85 show the 2.8-second nominal scroll opening. The two rectangular tablet panes use `scrollPose` and `railAssembly` geometry with the code's initial targets: center face-local X = ±1.6, width 1.2, height `1.2 × 900/546 = 1.9780`. Each pane has a named proposed center pivot Empty and two thin dowel references (radius .013, span 1.274). The browser recalculates tablet targets from viewport, camera, settings panels and manual zoom; these reference endpoints are **nominal**, not a universal live maximum. Tablet rectangles and dowels are simple clearance proxies, not cloth, rails, rollers or armature solids. Their stored sheet is hidden until unrolling, matching the runtime.

The `FOCUS VIEW ONLY` parent illustrates a cube turn from an arbitrary away angle to a Notes-facing view across frames 1–91. Pause at frame 91 for focused modeling. To remove the illustrative turn, select that parent, clear its animation data, then press Alt-R to reset rotation. The rotation is intentionally separate from the cube-local Notes mount and glass lift: focus rotates the entire cube toward the camera, while the physical lift follows the open/facing state. Camera-compensating pane roll and viewport-dependent floating tablet fallback are omitted. Guide empties and the opening circle are measurement aids, not proposed hardware or export geometry. The origin marker is a central pivot *reference*, not an engineering location for a new rail pivot.

Rebuild from repository root with Blender 4.5: `blender -b -t 2 --python _1-about/notes/blender-reference/build_reference.py`. `verification.json` records measured endpoints and sample poses. No site runtime or canonical GLB is changed.
