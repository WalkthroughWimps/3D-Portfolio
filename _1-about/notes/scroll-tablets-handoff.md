# Notes mechanical scroll tablets — October 18, 2026

Implements the user's circular lifting frame, four mechanical hands and opposing tablet scrolls. This supersedes the separate floating Layers/Gear stack presentation. The drawing document, grouping/pane model, Undo history and storage schema are unchanged. Existing saved drawings remain normalized to the circular pane and fit the inset glass without migration.

## Implementation

- `scroll-tablet-model.js` owns deterministic deployment, viewport layout, arm/spindle poses and the projected-rectangle homography. The original 2.2-second continuous-focus dwell remains. Opening takes 1.45 seconds: lift first, move the rolled devices outward, come forward around the pane, then unroll. Left feeds downward from its upper spindle; right feeds upward from its lower spindle. Screen controls remain hidden/inert until fully unspooled. Turning away blanks them immediately and stows the mechanism in .52 seconds. Reduced motion snaps to the final pose after the dwell.
- `mechanical-scrolls.js` owns a circular bezel/retaining annulus, four guides, four telescoping two-link arms, articulated three-finger/thumb grippers, spinning keyed spindles and rolled display sleeves. Shared low-poly geometries and instanced parts keep the mechanism to at most 13 draw batches; fully opened sleeves stop drawing. No shadows, DOM-to-texture copying, extra production render loop or added dependency. Instance buffers and projected DOM attributes remain untouched when settled.
- The source chassis was sampled in the Notes front band: minimum sampled opening radius .8950745. The inset glass radius is .835; the frame seats .045 in front of the old pane origin and extends a further .18. Folded arm/spindle envelopes fit the .895 design opening before outward travel. Sampled link-to-pane clearance is at least .01604 model units across the tested layouts; gripper envelopes are also checked. The annular bezel and covered seat intentionally meet the chassis. These samples are a visual route check, not a continuous triangle/self-collision or manufacturing proof.
- `notes-face.js` mounts all added geometry on the same lifted upright carrier as the glass, rear art, saved art and clipped continuations. A perspective HTML overlay matches each rendered screen; native buttons, sliders, inputs and layers stay native. Invalid/behind-camera screen quads hide and become inert. Layers shares the right screen with drawing tools, with a 10px grid gap and dividing rule. At limited widths the two sections stack within the same scroll, and the two scrolls shorten to leave drawing glass available below. Gear art remains on the left.
- The face-owned entry forwards `afterRender` so projection uses the shared host's final camera/settings framing. Edge layout preserves manual zoom; fitting stays opt-in and disabled while the backing is on. UI matrices cache by camera/carrier/pose, and the ordinary edge mode skips rim-fit sampling. No host, registry, schema, model or unrelated face source was edited.
- The briefly reserved shared `faces/notes-projector.js` now uses the existing `projectionSource` carrier and radius to extend its cone to the moving, inset glass. The API shape remains `{texture, carrier, radius}`. Current physical radius is .835; the projector borrows the same canvas texture and does not allocate another drawing surface.
- Scroll width/arm reach adapt to viewport and zoom for readable controls. The spindle telescopes during deployment. Fixed actuator lengths, travel stops and real hardware dimensions remain a later physical design task. Mechanical Creature Kit was not found in the installed Blender addons; this implementation uses procedural reusable parts.
