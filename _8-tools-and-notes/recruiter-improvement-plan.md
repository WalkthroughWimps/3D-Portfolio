## Sticky-note content and publishing

Author drafts stay in the browser. Export/import must preserve surface anchors and all appearance/content fields, with a versioned schema and validation. Visitor read/dismiss history is separate from author content. A reviewed export becomes the repository's published annotation data; local edits do not automatically change the live site. Notes should stay attached to a single meaningful surface and disappear when it is hidden or occluded. Do not allow an attachment to jump to an unrelated surface after a model change; report stale/unresolved anchors in Constructor.
