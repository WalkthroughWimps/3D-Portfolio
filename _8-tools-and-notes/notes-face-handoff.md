## Future gear-art direction

Prefer a curated circular small-gear set compatible with every supported large guide. Shared tooth pitch, integer tooth counts, gentle corner radii and clearance define the compatibility matrix. Start with circular guides and circular pinions; add rounded triangle, rounded square and oval guides only after checking their whole rolling path. Noncircular rollers need their own contact/closure solver and cannot be presumed universally compatible.

The gear drawer may close during guide changes, but a universal curated set remains the goal. Guide shapes should eventually rise from the glass as though precisely cut into it and vanish flush when parked; preserve interchangeable guide panes plus an uncut drawing pane. The document stays independent of pane variants. Actual cut glass, strength, tolerances, travel, mounting and manufacture remain unresolved.
