## Cause and repair

The old intro had several independent terminal paths. Muted decoder priming could consume a one-shot `playing` listener and race a real Play click; startup and post-click deadlines plus a 12-second no-progress watchdog could call completion; media errors also completed the intro. Each completion hid the video and started the tablet drop. Sound was created only when setup-time permission allowed it, then its source/metadata/start was deferred across async work, the Play promise was ignored, and an early flag suppressed later retries. `sound=1` also counted as permission. Videos grid creation separately scheduled a second tablet-animation fetch and began preview/full warmups on page load; a second page queue started tasks both in the queue and outside it. The worker cached a 206 as if it were complete and converted `cache.put` errors into request failures; cached range requests returned a full 200.

The focused repair changes:

- `_2-videos/videos.js` now has one terminal path: genuine `ended` or explicit Skip/S. Loading, buffering, pause, hidden-tab suspension, media errors, and rejected play promises do not complete the visual intro. It removes decoder priming and completion/watchdog deadlines, uses `readyState` evidence for readiness, exposes Retry/Skip after slow or failed startup, disables a pending duplicate Play, and disposes intro-owned listeners, timers, and consent/settings subscriptions on terminal completion. The existing tablet pose, camera, materials, lighting, 1,200 ms drop, and final visual handoff are retained.
- The same owner prepares the separate Opus soundtrack after consent, initiates audio and video on the Play gesture, handles audio play rejection and consent revocation, subscribes to saved mute/volume/sync changes, follows `max(0, videoTime - syncMs / 1000)`, matches playback rate, and realigns after pauses/buffering. Positive sync keeps audio muted until its timeline target is valid; a short remaining audio tail may continue after video `ended` so positive sync does not cut the soundtrack off at the visual handoff. The `sound=1` query no longer bypasses consent for the Videos grid or intro.
- `_2-videos/videos.html` adds a “Continue silently” recovery control for soundtrack failures; Skip placement is unchanged.
- `_7-shared-scripts/video-player-controls.js` now defers Videos grid scheduling until the intro terminal callback, removes its second tablet-animation warmup, and bounds preview/audio then full-video warming with single-invocation queues. Other pages keep their existing automatic scheduling.
- `sw.js` advances the owned cache to `intro-cache-v4`, stores only complete 200 responses, treats cache operations as best effort for successful network responses, passes online Range responses through without caching, and slices verified complete cached bodies for bounded, open-ended, and suffix single ranges. Unsatisfiable and unsupported multiple ranges return 416 with `Content-Range: bytes */size`. The intro remains the only worker-cached media asset.
- `tools/phase-02-correctness.test.mjs` adds handler regressions for online partial responses, cache write failure, cached range variants, 416, and versioned cache ownership. `tools/videos-intro-worker.mjs` and `tools/videos-intro-browser.mjs` are focused disposable validation runners.

No Videos CSS, tablet asset, generated worker config, shared consent implementation, public route, settings key/default, or unrelated page behavior was changed.
